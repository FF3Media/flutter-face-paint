Why It Works

Why Face Painting Works for Corporate Events

Face painting is one of the few activations that creates genuine, spontaneous moments of joy at corporate events. It draws a crowd, breaks the ice between colleagues, and — crucially — generates a flood of shareable social media content that extends your event's reach long after the last guest leaves.

For brand activations, Maryam can incorporate your brand colours and logo motifs directly into designs, turning every painted face into a living, walking piece of brand content. It's unexpected, it's photogenic, and guests absolutely love it.

Family-friendly and inclusive, face painting welcomes guests of all ages — making it the perfect activity for events where employees bring their children, or where you want every attendee, from the CEO to the newest hire, to feel genuinely welcomed and entertained.
