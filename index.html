Child Safety

Safe for Every Child

Every product we use is professionally tested and approved for sensitive skin.

  • FDA-approved, professional-grade face paints
  • Fresh sponge used per child — always
  • Hypoallergenic formulas safe for sensitive skin
  • Children who appear unwell are never painted
  • Full sanitation kit on-site at every event
